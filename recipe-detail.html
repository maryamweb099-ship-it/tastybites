ITALIAN

Garlic Bread

Crispy bread topped with garlic butter and herbs. Perfect as a snack or side dish.

15 min Prep & Cook
2 People Servings

Ingredients

  • 4 bread slices
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
  • 2 tablespoons grated cheese (optional)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Mix butter, chopped garlic and parsley.
  2. Spread the mixture over the bread slices.
  3. Add grated cheese if desired.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 8–10 minutes until golden.

ITALIAN STYLE

Cheesy Macaroni

Delicious macaroni cooked with cheese and creamy sauce for a comforting meal.

25 min Prep & Cook
2 People Servings

Ingredients

  • 200g macaroni
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • Salt and black pepper

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil macaroni according to the packet instructions.
  2. Melt butter in a pan and add flour.
  3. Gradually add milk and stir until thick.
  4. Add cheese, salt and black pepper.
  5. Mix in the macaroni and serve warm.

ITALIAN

Chicken Alfredo

Creamy pasta with tender chicken, garlic and Parmesan cheese.

30 min Prep & Cook
2 People Servings

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 150g boneless chicken
  • 1/2 cup cooking cream
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil pasta according to the packet instructions.
  2. Cut chicken into small pieces and cook thoroughly in olive oil.
  3. Add garlic and cook briefly.
  4. Add cream, Parmesan cheese, salt and pepper.
  5. Mix in the cooked pasta and serve warm.

ITALIAN

Cheesy Pizza

Homemade pizza topped with tomato sauce, melted cheese and fresh toppings.

30 min Prep & Cook
2 People Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 ready-made pizza base
  • 3 tablespoons pizza sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup sliced capsicum
  • 1/4 cup sliced olives
  • Oregano to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Place the pizza base on a baking tray.
  2. Spread pizza sauce over the base.
  3. Add mozzarella cheese and toppings.
  4. Sprinkle oregano on top.
  5. Bake according to the base instructions until the cheese melts.

DESSERT

Sweet Dessert

A simple, creamy dessert made with biscuits, whipped cream and chocolate.

15 min Preparation
2 People Servings

Ingredients

  • 8 plain biscuits
  • 1 cup whipped cream
  • 2 tablespoons chocolate syrup
  • 1 tablespoon chocolate chips

Cooking Instructions

  1. Crush the biscuits into small pieces.
  2. Add a layer of crushed biscuits to a serving glass.
  3. Add a layer of whipped cream.
  4. Repeat the layers and add chocolate syrup.
  5. Chill before serving.

HEALTHY

Healthy Bowl

A fresh bowl of vegetables and chickpeas with a simple lemon dressing.

15 min Preparation
2 People Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup lettuce
  • 1/2 cucumber, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1/2 cup boiled chickpeas
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper

Cooking Instructions

  1. Wash and chop the fresh vegetables.
  2. Place vegetables and chickpeas in a bowl.
  3. Mix olive oil, lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  4. Pour the dressing over the ingredients.
  5. Mix gently and serve fresh.
